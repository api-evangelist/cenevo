Waste in the lab rarely looks like waste. It appears as samples stored “just in case,” reagents reordered because no one is sure what’s already available, and experiments repeated due to incomplete or unreliable data. Over time, these small inefficiencies accumulate into a significant operational and financial burden, one that is often hidden in plain sight.
