Gene therapy consists of complex, multi-step processes involving living cells and viral vectors, and is significantly more variable than other drug production processes. This complexity means that strict standardized operating procedures (SOPs) are essential at every stage of the process. It’s also crucial to closely monitor each phase to maximize efficiency and quality, allowing labs to adjust the environment to prevent deviations in cell culture conditions.
